# Air Fryer Salmon

Dijon-coated salmon with paprika and garlic, done in the air fryer in about 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 4 salmon fillets (about 6 oz each)
- 2 Tbsp Dijon mustard
- 1 tsp paprika (sweet or smoked)
- 1 tsp garlic powder
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- 1/4 tsp black pepper
- Olive oil spray

## Steps

1. Set the air fryer to 400°F. Pat the fillets dry.
2. Spread about 1 tsp Dijon thinly over the top of each fillet.
3. Season the tops with salt and pepper, then paprika and garlic powder. Spray lightly with olive oil.
4. Air fry two fillets at a time for 8–10 minutes, until the fish flakes with a fork. Check at 8 minutes; repeat with the rest.

## Notes

- 2026-11-14: Works with king, coho, sockeye or steelhead. Skin can stay on (cook skin side down). Time depends on fillet thickness and the air fryer. Keeps 3 days in the fridge; reheat gently.
