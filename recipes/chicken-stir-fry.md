# Chicken Stir Fry

Chicken and crisp vegetables in a honey-soy sauce. About 20 minutes.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breast (or thighs), 1-inch cubes
- Salt and pepper
- 2 Tbsp olive oil, divided
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1/2 yellow bell pepper, 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 red bell pepper, 1-inch pieces
- 1/2 cup baby carrots, sliced
- 2 tsp minced ginger
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- **Sauce:** 1 Tbsp cornstarch
- **Sauce:** 2 Tbsp cold water
- **Sauce:** 1/4 cup low-sodium chicken broth
- **Sauce:** 3 Tbsp low-sodium soy sauce
- **Sauce:** 1/4 cup honey
- **Sauce:** 1 Tbsp toasted sesame oil
- **Sauce:** 1/2 tsp red pepper flakes (optional)
- To serve: rice and/or chow mein

## Steps

1. Sauce: whisk cornstarch into the cold water, then whisk in broth, soy, honey, sesame oil and pepper flakes. Set aside.
2. Heat 1 Tbsp oil in a large skillet or wok over medium-high. Season chicken and cook (in batches if needed) 3–5 minutes until cooked through. Remove.
3. Turn heat to medium, add the other 1 Tbsp oil and the broccoli, peppers and carrots; cook, stirring now and then, until crisp-tender.
4. Add ginger and garlic for 1 minute.
5. Return the chicken, re-whisk the sauce and pour it in. Bring to a boil and boil 1 minute to thicken.
6. Serve over rice or chow mein.

## Notes

- 2026-11-14: Servings not given on the page. Fresh vegetables beat frozen mixes. Readers suggest giving broccoli and carrots a few minutes' head start before the peppers. Other veg that work: snap peas, green beans, mushrooms, onion, zucchini. Top with cashews or sesame seeds.
