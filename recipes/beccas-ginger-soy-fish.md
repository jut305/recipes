# Becca's Ginger-Soy Fish

Cornstarch-dusted white fish pan-fried with crisp ginger and finished in a quick soy sauce. Serve over rice.

## Ingredients

- 12 oz halibut, cod or other white fish
- 1–2 Tbsp cornstarch
- 2 Tbsp cooking oil
- 2-inch piece ginger, peeled, cut into thin strips
- 4–6 scallions, chopped
- **Sauce:** 2 Tbsp soy sauce
- **Sauce:** 2 Tbsp water
- **Sauce:** 1 Tbsp sugar
- **Sauce:** 1 tsp sesame oil
- **Sauce:** 3 dashes white pepper
- To serve: rice

## Steps

1. Cut the fish into thick bite-size pieces and coat with cornstarch.
2. Mix the sauce until the sugar dissolves.
3. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan or wok over medium-high.
4. Stir-fry the ginger until light brown; remove and set aside.
5. Pan-fry the fish until just cooked and lightly browned.
6. Add the sauce; as soon as it bubbles, take it off the heat.
7. Serve over rice, topped with the fried ginger and scallions.
