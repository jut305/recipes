# Baked Salmon with Brown Sugar and Panko

A whole fillet with Dijon, a sweet-spicy rub and a buttery parsley-panko crust, baked hot on foil.

## Ingredients

- 1 1/2 lb skin-on salmon fillet, center-cut if possible
- 1 Tbsp Dijon mustard
- **Rub:** 2 Tbsp light brown sugar
- **Rub:** 1/2 tsp paprika
- **Rub:** 1/2 tsp garlic powder
- **Rub:** 1/4 tsp cayenne
- **Rub:** 1 tsp kosher salt, plenty of black pepper
- **Topping:** 1/4 cup panko
- **Topping:** 1/2 cup parsley leaves, chopped
- **Topping:** 2 Tbsp unsalted butter, melted
- **Topping:** 1/4 tsp kosher salt, a few grinds of pepper

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 425°F and line a sheet pan with foil.
2. Mix the rub in one bowl and the topping in another.
3. Lay the salmon skin side down on the foil and spread the Dijon over the top.
4. Press on the rub, then the panko topping. Crimp the foil up around the fish to catch the juices.
5. Bake 15–18 minutes, until the crumbs are golden and the fish flakes when pressed. Cut into 4 portions.

## Notes

- 2026-11-14: Start checking at 15 minutes (145°F per FDA; many prefer 125–140°F). Asparagus or potatoes can roast on a second sheet alongside. Keeps 3 days in the fridge; reheat at 275°F for 10–15 minutes. Not recommended for freezing.
