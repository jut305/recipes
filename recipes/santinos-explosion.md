# Santino's Explosion

Crumb-baked chicken cubed and tossed with angel hair, Parmesan, butter and Italian seasoning.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb boneless skinless chicken breasts
- 2 egg whites, beaten
- 3/4 cup Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs
- 2 lb angel hair pasta, cooked
- 1 cup grated Parmesan
- 1/2 cup liquid butter-style spread (squeeze bottle)
- 2 Tbsp dried Italian seasoning
- 1 Tbsp garlic pepper (?)

## Steps

1. Heat the oven to 350°F.
2. Dip the chicken in egg white, then coat in breadcrumbs.
3. Bake about 15 minutes per side, until cooked through and golden. Cut into bite-sized cubes.
4. Meanwhile, cook the angel hair and drain.
5. In a large bowl, toss the pasta with the chicken, Parmesan, liquid butter, Italian seasoning and pepper.
