# Pan-Seared Halibut with Lemon Caper Sauce

Butter-seared halibut finished with a quick pan sauce of garlic, white wine, lemon and capers.

## Ingredients

- 1 lb halibut, skinned, cut into 2 portions (thick end of the fillet is best)
- 4 Tbsp butter, divided
- 1 Tbsp extra-virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp kosher salt
- Black pepper
- 3–4 lemon slices (Meyer or regular)
- 2 tsp garlic, finely chopped
- 1/4 cup white wine (e.g. Chardonnay)
- 2 Tbsp lemon juice
- 2 Tbsp capers, drained
- 2 Tbsp flat-leaf parsley, chopped

## Steps

1. Lightly salt the top of the fish.
2. Heat 2 Tbsp butter and the oil in a skillet over medium. When the butter just starts to brown, add the fish top side down; season the other side with the rest of the salt and pepper. Cook about 5 minutes, then flip carefully.
3. Add the lemon slices and cook 3–5 minutes more, until it just flakes in the center. Pull it slightly underdone — it keeps cooking. Move fish and lemons to a plate.
4. Pour off all but about 2 Tbsp clean fat. Over medium, cook the garlic 1 minute, add the wine and let it reduce, then add lemon juice, capers and parsley for 1 minute.
5. Off the heat, stir in the remaining 2 Tbsp butter. Return the fish and juices, spoon sauce over, warm 30 seconds, and serve with the lemon slices.

## Notes

- 2026-11-14: Cod is softer and falls apart with this method; if using it, cook less than half as long. 1 lb makes two generous portions.
